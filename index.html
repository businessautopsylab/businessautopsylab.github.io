Business Autopsy Publisher - Privacy Policy

Business Autopsy Publisher is a private tool used only by the owner of the Business Autopsy YouTube channel to upload and manage videos on that channel via the YouTube Data API.

The app accesses only the owner's own YouTube account. It does not collect, store, sell or share personal data of any other users. OAuth tokens are stored locally on the owner's computer and are used solely to publish and update the channel's videos.

Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
